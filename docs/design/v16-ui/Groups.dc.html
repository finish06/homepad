Groups: hierarchy and rhythm
A group header is one bold word. It cannot be collapsed, it does not say how many apps are inside, and it says nothing about whether any of them are down — so on a long dashboard the user scrolls the whole page to find the one red tile.

Today — boxes hug their content and wrap
dead space
Each box is as wide as its app count, so rows break wherever the next box stops fitting. Group sizes are an accident of how many apps you happen to have.
Proposed — snap to a 12-column grid
A box picks a span — 3, 4, 6 or 12 columns — and rows share a baseline. Tiles keep their fixed 190px track inside, so nothing stretches; the box just stops being sized by accident.
