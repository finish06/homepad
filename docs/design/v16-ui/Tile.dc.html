Anatomy
Icon plate keeps its 12px radius and tinted backing, so monochrome logos still read as deliberate. The name drops from 15px/600 centred with two lines reserved to 13.5px/600 left-aligned on one, which is what buys the status line. That second line is the whole point: it is the only place a user learns a service is slow before they click it. The dot holds a fixed right rail so a column of tiles scans as a column of dots.
